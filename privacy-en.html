Beat The Competition – Privacy Policy

Effective: 12 November 2026 Controller: Pál Norbert, private individual, Romania Privacy contact: beatthecompetitiondev@gmail.com

This policy applies to the Beat The Competition mobile game, game accounts and online game services. You can use a guest account; linking a Google account is optional.

The current version contains no advertisements or real-money in-app purchases. Packs and virtual items can be acquired using virtual currency earned in the game. If this changes, we will update this policy and the app’s privacy information before the change.

Local acceptance record: The game stores the accepted document/beta-notice version and UTC acceptance time on your device so it does not ask again for the same version. This record is not sent to the backend. It remains until app data is cleared or replaced by a later acceptance. We do not store a date of birth or the parent’s identity through this screen.

Connection latency: During online matches, short network messages measure and display round-trip latency in milliseconds. Samples are held temporarily in memory; the client does not create persistent test reports or copyable diagnostic files from them. Server-side connection checks and technical logs needed to operate and protect multiplayer are separate.

Google account: Linking is optional. The game uses the identifier needed to identify the Google account; it does not request access to its email address or other account data, or store a Google password. The linked identifier is retained while the game account exists; short-lived sign-in flows are removed after expiry.

Visibility to other players: Player names and club abbreviations may be visible in searches, friend lists and matches. Do not use a real name, contact details or unnecessary personal information as your player name. There is no text or voice chat and no image or file upload.

Online connections: The game uses WebRTC. Direct player-to-player connections can expose network addressing information, including your public IP address, to the other player’s device. If a direct connection cannot be established, traffic may pass through a TURN relay, which processes technical connection data and relayed game traffic. A STUN provider used for setup may also process network addressing information.

2. Age group

The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and above. Its Terms of Use require players to be at least 13; minors need the approval and acceptance of a parent or legal guardian as required by applicable law. Content ratings such as PEGI are separate. The entry screen asks for a declaration concerning these requirements, but does not request a date of birth or independently verify age or parental authority. It therefore cannot guarantee that no under-13 player uses the service.

Parents or legal guardians who wish to raise a privacy request about a child’s account can email us. We will review the request and act under applicable law. Account deletion can also be initiated in the app.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the game and account: Processing accounts, profiles, saved progress, virtual goods, friend features and match data is necessary to provide the service requested by the player (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Local acceptance records support this service and its versioned terms.
  • Security and abuse prevention: Necessary authentication, session and technical data protect accounts and the service. The basis is our legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); you may object to such processing.
  • Support: Necessary correspondence is processed on the basis of our legitimate interests in responding (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Where necessary to fulfil a legal obligation, such as handling an erasure request, the basis is that obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c)).

Acknowledging this policy is not general consent to data processing.

5. Retention and deletion

Account data: We keep profiles, progress, virtual goods and associated online data while the account exists. Accounts are not automatically deleted for inactivity.

Beta reset: Beta progress and virtual goods will be reset at production launch, as stated in the Terms of Use. A game-progress reset is distinct from an account erasure request and does not itself promise deletion of authentication data, technical logs or backups. Their retention follows this policy.

Account deletion: Start deletion under Settings → Account → Delete club. Deleting a Google-linked account requires Google authentication to confirm the operation. Deletion removes the player’s profile, game data, relationships, invitations, match data and sessions from the active database. The record necessary to safely complete the deletion expires 24 hours after creation and is subsequently removed by regular cleanup.

If you cannot access the app, email beatthecompetitiondev@gmail.com. We request only proportionate information needed to verify authority over the account; we do not request passwords or login tokens by email. Uninstalling the app does not itself delete the server account.

Data removed from the active database may temporarily remain in earlier backups. Daily backups are hidden 7 days after upload and deleted after 1 further day; weekly backups are hidden after 28 days and deleted after 1 further day. Provider lifecycle processing may delay actual permanent deletion slightly.

Short-lived authentication records: A background process cleans expired sessions, Google sign-in flows and account-operation records every five minutes in limited batches while the server runs. After downtime, sleeping servers or temporary errors, cleanup resumes when service is restored.

Service logs: Under the current account configuration, Render application logs are available for 7 days. GitHub Actions logs support workflow verification and follow the hosting provider’s and repository’s log retention settings; they do not contain database dump contents. TURN container logs on Hetzner are limited to 10 MB per file and 3 files per container. Older files are automatically removed when those limits are reached. There is no separate time-based deletion rule, so retention depends on log volume and can exceed 30 days. The VPS’s own system logs are separate.

Support correspondence: Messages are retained for the time necessary to answer and close the request. Necessary portions may be retained longer for legal obligations, evidence of handling privacy requests or legal claims.

6. Security and your rights

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards against unauthorized access, modification, loss and misuse. Authentication secrets and session tokens are stored as non-reversible hashes. Database backups are encrypted before upload, and access is protected by service secrets and restricted permissions.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and request data portability where its conditions are met. Email beatthecompetitiondev@gmail.com. Proportionate identity verification may be needed to protect accounts.

We respond without undue delay, normally within one month. Where justified, this may be extended by up to two further months; we will notify you and explain why within the first month.

You may complain to Romania’s data protection authority, ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro), or the competent EEA supervisory authority where you habitually reside, work or where the alleged infringement occurred.

7. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when processing, providers, game features or legal requirements materially change. Updated versions state their effective date, and players will receive appropriate notice of material changes.
