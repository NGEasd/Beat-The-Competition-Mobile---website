Account and Data Deletion

This page provides information for Beat The Competition players regarding the process of deleting their account and associated personal data, in compliance with Google Play's data safety guidelines.

1. Deleting Guest Accounts

Since guest accounts are not linked to an identifiable email address or password, for security reasons—to prevent others from deleting your account using a stolen ID—guest accounts can ONLY be deleted from within the app.

Steps:
  1. Open the Beat The Competition app.
  2. Go to the Settings menu.
  3. Tap the Delete Account button and confirm your intention.

Important: Please do this before uninstalling the app. Uninstalling the app will permanently remove your access to the guest account on your device, and your previous statistics will no longer be accessible.

2. Deleting Google-Linked Accounts

If you have linked the game to your Google account and have already uninstalled the app (or no longer have access to the device), you can request the permanent deletion of your account and saved data from our servers via email.

Sending a deletion request:

Send an email to the following address: beatthecompetitiondev@gmail.com

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Requirement: You MUST send the email from the exact email address you used to log into the game. This verifies that you are the rightful owner of the account.

Your request will be processed manually within 30 days. During this process, all data associated with you (profile, in-game progress, statistics) will be permanently and irreversibly deleted.
