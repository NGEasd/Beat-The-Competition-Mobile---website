Account and Data Deletion

This page provides information for Beat The Competition players regarding the process of deleting their account and associated personal data, in compliance with Google Play's data safety guidelines.

1. Primary Method: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest and most secure way to delete your account—whether you play as a Guest or use Google Sign-In—is directly within the game. This instantly removes your active profile from our database.

Steps:
  1. Open the Beat The Competition app.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your intention to permanently delete your data.

2. Secondary Method: Web / Email Request

If you have already uninstalled the app and cannot access your account (either Guest or Google-linked), you can initiate a deletion request via email.

How to request deletion:

Send an email to: beatthecompetitiondev@gmail.com

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Required Info: Please provide your exact in-game Player ID and Club Abbreviation.
  • Security Note: We may need additional information to locate the account and verify your authority before processing the deletion. Please do not send passwords or login tokens.

3. Deletion Scope and Timeframe

Once your deletion request is verified and processed (within 30 days):

  • Active Data: Your game account, progress, and virtual balances will be immediately and permanently deleted from our active database.
  • Logs and Backups: Temporary technical operation records will expire naturally. Data stored in secure server backups and provider logs may take additional time to be completely purged, subject to the retention cycles of our cloud providers.
